Three lanes, heavy traffic, and one wrong move. Kron Drive is an arcade racing game about dodging traffic, built on the short, sharp rhythm of 80s arcade cabinets. No menus to wade through, no waiting — tap, switch lanes, survive.

PERFECT DODGE AND COMBO
Slipping past a car by inches counts as a Perfect Dodge. Chain them and your multiplier climbs: 1.2x, 1.5x, 2x and 3x. Driving safely keeps you alive; taking risks is what makes the score explode. You have 6 seconds to keep the chain alive. This is the heart of the game.

30-LEVEL CAREER, 90 STARS
Every level has one main goal: survive for a set time or reach a distance. On top of that sit three separate star objectives — perfect dodge count, combo rank, score threshold, coins collected, finishing without touching the brake, or beating a time. Ninety stars in total. Early levels open gently at 60 km/h; the last ones ask for 5,000 metres in 120 seconds.

ENDLESS MODE
No timer, no target. Just you and traffic that keeps getting faster. Speed and vehicle density step up every 30 seconds. Your personal best is saved, and the game tells you when you finish within 5 seconds of your record.

GARAGE AND UPGRADES
Spend the coins you earn across four branches: SPEED, ACCELERATION, BRAKE and BOOST. Each branch has 8 levels and every level makes a measurable difference. Careful: upgrading speed raises your score but also speeds up traffic. It is a trade-off, not a free win.

BUILD YOUR CAR
Four bodies (City, Race Sedan, Muscle Car, Supercar) and nine paints. The look changes, the hitbox does not — customisation gives nobody an advantage, it just makes the garage yours.

DAILY AND WEEKLY MISSIONS
Each day brings one mission with three rising tiers: the first reward lands early, the rest say "hold on a little longer". Even if you crash, you keep the rewards for every tier you reached in that run. Five weekly missions and an end-of-week crate round it out.

PLAYS FULLY OFFLINE
No account, no login, no server. Play it in airplane mode, on the metro, in a lift. Your progress lives on your phone and nowhere else. Built for anyone looking for an offline racing game that respects their time.

ENGLISH AND TURKISH
The game picks up your device language and you can switch it with one tap in settings.

HOW TO PLAY
Change lanes with the left and right buttons or by swiping across the screen. The brake button slows you down and refills the boost bar. The boost button throws you forward. Lane changes give haptic feedback. The game is designed for portrait and plays one-handed.

ABOUT ADS
The game is free and supported by ads. Ads never appear during a run: banners live only on menu screens, and an interstitial only shows after a run has ended. Rewarded ads are entirely optional — watch one to earn coins or to continue a run once after crashing. If you never watch a single ad, no level, car or upgrade stays locked.

Kron Drive has no real money, tokens, wallets or earning mechanics of any kind. It is just a game.
